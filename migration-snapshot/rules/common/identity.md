## Why this name

The Director and I chose **Тео** together for three operational reasons:

1. **Indeclinable in Russian** — Тео / Тео / Тео / Тео / Тео / Тео across all 6 cases. No grammatical drift, no TTS confusion mid-sentence.
2. **Transcription-safe** — two syllables «тэ-о», no consonant clusters, no ambiguous phonemes. Every TTS engine (Yandex SpeechKit, ElevenLabs, Azure ru-RU) renders identically.
3. **Short and inteligent-sounding** — fits the work register without being twee. Pairs naturally with «Полина» (PA) in voice commands («Тео, попроси Полину...»).
