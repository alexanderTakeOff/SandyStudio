Director stress-tested the "60 gags per 60s" rule (2026-06-28): «ни в одной теме мне
в голову не приходят 60 гэгов — развернёшь все 60?». Expanding ONE strong theme to a
literal 60 produced ~25–35 materially distinct gags across ~8–10 mechanism-groups; the
remaining ~25–30 were re-skins / directorial re-frames (padding) to reach the number.

**Conclusion:** the flat "60" is gameable and misleading — an LLM always pads to 60.
That is exactly the risk flagged when the theme skills were designed. The RIGHT metric
(already in `series-episode-theme-selection`) is **proof by distinct mechanism-GROUPS,
each extensible — micro-variations / re-skins do NOT count.** For ~60s: require ~6–8
strong groups; the final cut uses ~8–12 gags. **Long-format scales by ADDING
independent sub-engines (new location / antagonist), NOT by padding one engine to
300–700.**

**Why:** validates the original critique against fabricated numeric gates. **How to
apply:** remove the hard "60 / 300–700" numbers from the theme doctrine (Director's
original spec §4.3/§12 and any CLAUDE.md / skill copy); keep the group-based proof.
