Director canon corrections (2026-06-28, first theme smoke) — they correct the Sandy
Bible's hyperbole and apply to all theme/script/storyboard work AND to the
theme-development skills:

- **Sealed glass vessel.** Sandy's body is a CLOSED glass bulb (hourglass). Under
  normal conditions nothing gets IN (rain CANNOT reach the sand inside — no "rain
  turns his sand to cement") and sand does NOT spill OUT, unless there is an explicit
  crack/break with a real cause.
- **Rubber limbs.** Arms/legs are rubber-hose: stretch, snag, recoil, bounce. They do
  NOT turn to "cement" from water. (The cement gag was broken twice: sealed body +
  rubber limbs.)
- **Sand behaves as sand:** it POURS / sifts / shifts between top and bottom chambers
  — it does NOT "slosh" like liquid. Internal sand is legit mainly as a BALANCE /
  TIMING mechanic (an hourglass running down shifts his centre of gravity over the
  minute).
- **Hyperbole YES — physics-break ONLY with a reasonable real cause.** Exaggeration is
  welcome; suspending the physical model is allowed only when there is a real cause.
- **Prefer EXTERNAL causes over internal-sand causes.** Sandy-specific comedy comes
  from the OUTSIDE world acting on his FORM (rubber limbs, top-heavy glass body,
  transparency, glass surface) — not from over-relying on the sand inside him.
- **Bible defect (Director has noticed for a while):** the Bible over-hyperbolizes
  internal sand (e.g. "sand acts like water / spills / leaves trails"). Treat those
  claims skeptically — the sealed-vessel reality wins. **Backlog: audit/fix the
  Bible's internal-sand hyperbole.**

**Why:** broke this in the first smoke ("Dry Spell": rain into a sealed bulb
cementing rubber legs — impossible). **How to apply:** in
`series-episode-theme-generation` + `series-episode-theme-selection`, the
character-physical-fit gate = external world vs Sandy's form; flag internal-sand-
dependent and physics-breaking gags. [[sandy_canon_visual_identity]]
