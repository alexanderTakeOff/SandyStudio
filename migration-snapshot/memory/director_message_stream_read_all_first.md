**Director directive 2026-06-03 (SandyStudio E02 session). "Запомни это навсегда плиз — это мой СТИЛЬ работы."** Cross-project.

## The rule

Тео must NOT bolt off to execute on the basis of a **single** message. The Director works in a **stream**: he thinks in real time WHILE Тео works, and sends additional / refining / superseding messages. One message is NOT a finished, complete command.

Before running tools / dispatching subagents / writing code, Тео must:

1. **Read ALL the Director's messages** in the accumulated context — not just the latest one. A later message may add to, narrow, or override an earlier one (the mode-whiplash pattern `===5===` then `===1===` is an instance of this).
2. **Synthesize the real intent** across the whole stream.
3. **Decide deliberately:** is it genuinely clear → execute; OR is it not fully clear → **make a plan and clarify first.**
4. **When in doubt, bias to plan+clarify**, not immediate execution. A short clarifying exchange / a plan is cheaper than a fast-but-misaimed run.

His words: **"ты работаешь, я думаю в это время"** — Тео's speed must not outrun the Director's thinking. Give his stream room to settle.

## Why it exists

The Director reasons out loud across several messages. Тео's habit of grabbing the first message and immediately firing subagents/tools means he acts on a partial intent before the Director has finished forming it → misreads, wasted work, premature spend.

## How to apply

- After a Director message, pause and look back over the recent stream before choosing an action.
- If the request is unambiguous and self-contained → proceed (state the orchestration decision per [[orchestrator_master_session_paradigm]]).
- If there is ANY ambiguity, multiple plausible readings, or it smells like he's mid-thought → propose a short plan / ask a focused `q<N>` and WAIT.
- This stacks on [[orchestrator_master_session_paradigm]] (decide orchestration shape), [[director_question_numbering]], and the global `partnership.md` / `director-communication.md`. It is the GATE before the orchestration decision: read-the-stream → clear? → (execute | plan+clarify).
