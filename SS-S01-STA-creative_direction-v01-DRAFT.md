## Strategic Context

**Director intent:**
Silent physical comedy series. Protagonist is Sandy — a living hourglass made of soft
stretchable silicone filled with heavy gold sand. Physics is the language. Emotion is
expressed exclusively through the position and movement of mass. Zero dialogue. Zero text.

**Market opportunity (pre-launch baseline):**
AI-generated animation niche is early-stage and undersaturated with original IP.
Short-form (under 90 seconds) outperforms in discovery and completion rate.
Silent format removes the most common failure point of AI content (voice/lip sync).
Distinctive character design (hourglass physics) is immediately recognisable at thumbnail size.

**Brand alignment:**
SandyStudio produces original IP with a repeatable, scalable visual system.
Sandy the character is both the studio mascot and the franchise anchor.
Every episode validates the production pipeline, not just the content.

---

## Creative Vision

**Genre:** Silent physical comedy / animated short
**Comedy philosophy:**
Comedy comes from the gap between Sandy's self-image and what physics actually does to her.
She believes she controls the situation. Gravity always has the last word.
No character "feels" anything — they ARE their physical state. This is the only rule.

**Audience target:**
People who respond to visual wit over verbal punchlines. Animation fans who notice
craft. Short-form viewers who complete 60-second content. Adults and older teens.
Platform: YouTube Shorts + standard YouTube.

**Format parameters:**
```
episode_length_seconds: 60
episode_frequency_days: 7
series_arc_type:        anthology   # each episode standalone, recurring characters
```

## Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:**
Bold graphic modernism. Art Deco nightlife geometry. Smooth synthetic surfaces against
deep velvet darkness. Extreme squash-and-stretch on soft materials (Sandy).
Zero organic movement on hard surfaces (Inspector). Maximum contrast.

**Visual reference qualities** (describe aesthetic, not sources):
- The feel of a 1930s poster: high contrast, flat planes of colour, strong silhouettes
- Nightclub lighting aesthetic: single hard spotlight cone, deep cobalt fill, darkness as negative space
- Mechanical precision: right angles, clicks, no curves except Sandy herself

**Audio tone:**
No dialogue. No narration. Sound design is structural:
rubber stretching, sand cascading, brass clicking, crowd murmur as ambient texture.
Music: sparse and percussive, tracks Sandy's physical state not the drama.

**Character philosophy:**
Two characters. Maximum contrast. Sandy = organic, soft, self-deceiving.
Inspector = rigid, mechanical, incorruptible. Comedy from their contact.

**World philosophy:**
The club entrance is a social machine. It has one rule: appearances determine admission.
Sandy beats the rule through physics. Physics then enforces the rule anyway.

## Constraints

**Creative constraints:**
- Zero text on screen. Zero speech bubbles. Zero narration.
- Emotion expressed only through physics and mass distribution. Never facial expression alone.
- Sandy's sand position is always narratively meaningful — not decorative.

**Platform constraints (from youtube.md):**
- 60-second target → YouTube Shorts eligible (≤60s)
- selfDeclaredMadeForKids: false
