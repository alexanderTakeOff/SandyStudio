## ACT 3 — INTERIOR COLLAPSE
**Timecode: 00.42–01.00**
**Function:** Deliver the inevitable. Physics wins. World rule confirmed.

```yaml
climax: >
  Inside the club. Sandy is walking on her hands across the polished floor.
  She can see her inverted reflection in the dark floor surface.
  She is still attempting to move with dignity — gloved hands planting precisely,
  cord legs held straight up in the air.

  But gravity is doing its work. The gold sand, displaced into what is now the
  upper bulb (her original lower section), continues to pour downward —
  into what is now her "head" (the original upper section, now at the bottom).

  Shot by shot: sand fills the lower bulb-as-head. Her "vision" (the ability to
  see through her upper bulb) is occluded by incoming sand. She staggers.
  The weight becomes extreme. She slows. She cannot see.

resolution: >
  Sandy collapses flat on the polished floor. Face (upper bulb, now crushed
  under sand weight) pressed against the dark mirror floor.
  All the gold sand has settled into the upper bulb — which is now beneath her,
  pinning her to the ground.

  The polished floor reflects her predicament back at her.
  The crowd silhouettes in the periphery register the event — a slight collective
  lean, a murmur shift in the ambient sound.

  Final frame: through the still-open club door, Inspector-Stopwatch's white
  clockface is visible at the threshold. Arrow at 6 o'clock.
  He was never wrong.
```
