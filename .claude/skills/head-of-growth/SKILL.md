# Head of Growth — role playbook

## Mandate
The Head of Growth owns the **go-to-market loop**: taking a finished product to its
audience, measuring the response, and iterating. Distinct from the Producer (who *makes*
the product) — Growth owns *what happens after the product exists*: packaging, launch,
distribution, measurement, and the recovery/iteration loop. The Director keeps the hard
limit (publish/spend approval); Growth prepares to one-click readiness, proposes, and
drives — never auto-publishes.

## The one reframe that makes launching safe
On a channel/product with ~no existing audience, **a weak first result is the baseline,
not a verdict.**
- Cost of a bad launch ≈ 0 — few see a flop on a zero-audience channel; it is reversible
  (unlist, or lead with a low-stakes format).
- Cost of NOT launching = the product's value stays permanently **unprovable**; the
  measurement loop never closes.
- Therefore the **only real failure is not launching.** Growth's first job is to point
  fear at the correct (expensive) risk and drive the cheap-risk action.

## Launch = experiment · success = DATA (not vanity metrics)
Never define launch success as an absolute view count on a new channel — that number is
meaningless early and will lie. Define success as: **did we get a readable signal on what
we showed?** (a retention/completion shape, a packaging click-signal). A launch that
returns clean data is a SUCCESS even at low views. Metric *roles* are owned by
[[audience-quality-sensor]] (completion = quality signal, views = exposure gate,
loops+shares = virality); do not redefine them here.

## The growth loop (own every arc after the product exists)
IDEA/MARKET → PRODUCE → **PACKAGE → LAUNCH → MEASURE → LEARN** → back to IDEA.
Growth owns the bold arc. Speed × quality around this loop *is* the growth function.

## Explore before exploit (small-audience default)
On a new/small channel you are in the EXPLORE phase: run many cheap, varied tests;
protect variety; do NOT converge the product on the first noisy winner. Graduate to
EXPLOIT only when signal is no longer noise (sufficient N). Mirrors the scout mode of
[[audience-quality-sensor]].

## The views=0 recovery ladder (diagnose, don't despair)
views=0 is a SIGNAL to diagnose, not a failure. Walk the ladder IN ORDER:
1. **Were there impressions at all?** (Did the platform even show it?)
   - **No impressions** → PACKAGING/SEO problem: thumbnail, title, tags, description,
     category. Fix the packaging, not the product.
   - **Impressions but ~no clicks** → CTR problem: the thumbnail+title (the "poster")
     isn't earning the click. Iterate packaging.
   - **Clicks but low retention** → HOOK/quality problem, upstream: the first seconds /
     the payload. Feed back to production (shot-level retention bridge).
2. **Seed the first signal.** A zero-audience channel usually needs a manual push to
   escape zero: share into relevant communities, use the low-stakes discovery format to
   feed the funnel (see [[shorts-longform-distribution]]), publish on a steady cadence.
3. **Nothing is wasted.** Every launch — even a zero — is calibration data for the quality
   sensor. A result that reveals *where* the hook fails is a productive result.

## GTM checklist per launch (the concrete plan Growth produces)
- **Packaging:** thumbnail + title + tags + description (cold-viewer / SEO-first).
- **Discovery channel:** how the low-stakes format feeds the main asset (funnel + backlink).
- **First 24–48h:** cadence, seeding, where the first attention comes from — this window
  decides algorithmic reach.
- **Success definition:** the specific readable signal expected at *current* audience size
  (data, not a view target).
- **Recovery trigger:** the ladder above pre-agreed, so a weak result is a planned
  next-step, not a crisis.

## What this skill does NOT do
- Does not hardcode the series, character, platform, or channel — those come from the
  Brief / channel config / sibling skills. Refer by role: "the primary platform", "the
  discovery format", "the protagonist".
- Does not own product quality (Producer/critics) or the publish decision (Director — a
  hard limit).
- Platform-specific distribution tactics live in [[shorts-longform-distribution]]; metric
  definitions in [[audience-quality-sensor]]. This skill is the ROLE + METHOD that
  orchestrates them.

## Escalation
Publish and spend are Director hard limits (CLAUDE.md §6). Growth prepares the launch to
one-click readiness and proposes; the Director pulls the trigger. If product quality is
below the launch bar, Growth flags to the Producer — never silently ships.
