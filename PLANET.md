## The destination — a working Mode 3

## The Director's touchpoints (the gate map — Director 2026-06-27)
1. **Brief** — approve the idea.
2. **Script** — finalize the comedy (in words); cheap critics catch structure, the Director judges "funny".
3. **2 video pilots** — approve the first two REAL video shots: visual direction works + worth paying for the rest.
4. **Final cut → Publish** — review the whole episode, click Publish (a hard-limit, every mode).

**Auto-guardrail (no thought unless tripped):** the **budget ceiling** — if the episode would exceed the
limit it HALTS and calls the Director.

**Timing — resolved (Director 2026-06-27): NOT a separate touch → stays 4.** Shot timing folds into the
existing gates: at the **2-pilot checkpoint (gate 3)** the Director reviews the two real videos and can
glance-and-fix the timing feel there; the **final cut (gate 4)** is where the full timeline gets its last
look. No standalone video-editor gate.

## The reorder (Director optimization 2026-06-27 — substantially cheaper)
- **Old:** generate ALL references → review → video pilots.
- **New:** **2 refs → 2 video pilots from them → Director approves the 2 VIDEO shots → THEN generate the
  remaining refs → videos as refs complete.**
- **Wins:** (a) **money** — no blind pre-generation of the whole ref set; a rejected direction throws away 2
  shots, not the episode; (b) **one Director click** (video pilots) instead of two (ref-direction + video).
- **Ref-animatic batch review is GONE** → replaced by **per-reference canon-checking at generation**: ~80%
  pass automatically; a canon violation → **STOP, Director call**. Works for the 2 pilots and the second-wave refs.
