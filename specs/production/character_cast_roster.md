## Purpose

A 16-entry cast brainstorm from 2026-05-12: Sandy (the hero) plus 15
anthropomorphic candidates — everyday objects given a face/limbs/personality,
in the same design language as Sandy himself. **These are objects, not
humans** — this is the deliberate answer to "crowd/background characters
should not be generic human silhouettes."

## Canonization status

Only **CH_01 (Sandy)** is fully canonized (`SBL-character_sandy`, LEAD).
**CH_03 (Inspector-Stopwatch)** partially survives under the same name in the
currently-active `sandy-gag-library` §7 antagonist roster. **Everything else
below (CH_02, CH_04–CH_16) has never been canonized** — no `CHR-*`/`OBJ-*`
entity id, no Bible profile, no `specs/company/series_bible.md` mention. They
are strong, already-approved-in-spirit creative material sitting idle, not
active canon. Treat every non-Sandy/non-Inspector row below as **NOT YET
CANONIZED** until a Character Profile (`specs/schemas/character_profile.md`)
is authored and Director-approved.
