## ACT 1 — ARRIVAL

---

### SCENE 1 — THE ENTRANCE
**Location:** `club_exterior_entrance`
**Timecode:** 00.00–00.18

---

**BEAT 1** `00.00–00.08`

*The red carpet. Night. A hard white spotlight cone cuts the darkness.*
*The carpet runs from left frame toward heavy brass double doors.*
*Inspector-Stopwatch stands at his pedestal station, left of the velvet rope.*
*Arrow-brow: 3 o'clock. Neutral. He has seen everything before.*

*From the left edge: Sandy enters.*

*Sand: 100% in upper bulb.*
*Her silhouette is unmistakable — upper bulb round and full, lower bulb slim.*
*She walks with a swagger that belongs to someone with nowhere to be and no one to answer to.*
*The cord legs barely touch the carpet. The white gloves move ahead of her like advance scouts.*

*She does not look at Inspector. She doesn't need to.*

`SFX: T01_swagger begins — muted brass stab, light woodblock, walking bass`

---

**BEAT 2** `00.08–00.18`

*Sandy is three steps from the rope when it happens.*

*A piece of black sticky debris rolls across the carpet from the right.*
*It moves fast — a streak of viscous dark across deep red.*
*It catches Sandy's lower bulb as she steps forward.*

*Sandy does not notice. She is looking ahead.*

*The smear is there: irregular, black, iridescent. On her pale lower silicone.*
*Unmissable. The audience sees it. Sandy does not.*

*She continues her approach, completely unaware.*
*The walk is still a swagger. The smear travels with her.*

`SFX: 00.14 — trash_impact: soft wet smack`
`MUSIC: T01 cuts HARD on the smear frame. Silence.`

## ACT 2 — REJECTION AND SOLUTION

---

### SCENE 2 — THE SCAN
**Location:** `club_exterior_entrance`
**Timecode:** 00.18–00.33

---

**BEAT 3** `00.18–00.26`

*Sandy arrives at the rope. She stops. She looks at Inspector.*
*This is, in her estimation, a formality.*

*Inspector's shaft extends to scanning height.*
*Arrow-brow begins its slow clockwise spin: PROCESSING_SCAN.*

`SFX: 00.19 — inspector_scan_start: brass click + hydraulic hiss`

*The spin slows. It stops.*
*Arrow: 6 o'clock.*

*Inspector's shaft retracts to minimum height.*
*He glides — smooth, frictionless — sideways to block the rope.*

`SFX: 00.24 — inspector_reject: single descending chime`

*Sandy does not move. She looks down.*

*The smear is on her lower bulb. She can see it now.*
*She looks up at Inspector. Arrow: still at 6.*
*She looks at the rope. Blocked.*
*She looks back at Inspector.*

*Arrow: still at 6.*

`SFX: T02_assessment begins — pizzicato strings, sustained low brass`

---

**BEAT 4** `00.26–00.33`

*Sandy looks left. The crowd silhouettes in the darkness — they are watching.*
*She looks right. The brass double doors: shut.*
*She looks down at the smear. Up at Inspector. Down at the smear.*

*Something resolves in her posture.*
*The logic is simple: the dirty part is at the bottom.*
*Put the dirty part at the top.*

*Sandy plants her white gloved hands on the red carpet.*

`SFX: 00.30 — sandy_handstand: silicone stretch squeak + gloves on carpet`

*One clean inversion: she is upside down.*
*Lower bulb — smear and all — now high in the air.*
*Upper bulb — pale, clean, empty — now at the bottom, at Inspector's sensor height.*

*Sand: still mostly in the original upper bulb.*
*But it has begun to move. The laws are already working.*

---

### SCENE 3 — THE SECOND SCAN
**Location:** `club_exterior_entrance`
**Timecode:** 00.33–00.42

---

**BEAT 5** `00.33–00.42`

*Inspector reassesses.*
*His shaft extends again to the new scan height — the inverted lower bulb.*
*He sees: clean pale silicone. No smear.*

*Arrow-brow spins: PROCESSING_SCAN.*
*Then: a deliberate arc. Moving from 6 toward 12. One degree at a time.*

`SFX: 00.34 — sand_migration_start: low dry cascade, continuous`

*Arrow reaches 12 o'clock.*

`SFX: 00.40 — inspector_approve: single ascending chime`

*The rope parts.*
*Inspector glides back to his station.*

*Sandy — still on her hands, gloves on red carpet — walks through.*
*Step by step. Glove by glove.*
*The smeared lower bulb bobs above her, held high by her inverted legs.*
*The sand pours steadily toward what is now the lowest point.*

`MUSIC: T02 resolves — strings ascending, then cut on chime`

## ACT 3 — INTERIOR COLLAPSE

---

### SCENE 4 — THE WALK
**Location:** `club_interior`
**Timecode:** 00.42–00.56

---

**BEAT 6** `00.42–00.50`

*Inside. The polished dark floor reflects two spotlights from above.*
*Crowd silhouettes at the periphery. Ambient murmur.*

*Sandy enters, walking on her hands.*
*She attempts dignity: gloves plant precisely, cord legs held straight upward.*
*Her reflection mirrors her in the dark floor below — she can see herself.*

*But the reflection shows what she cannot feel yet:*
*the original upper bulb — now at the bottom, pressed against the floor — is filling.*
*Gold sand cascades steadily into it from above.*

*Sandy's "view" through the upper bulb begins to dim as sand accumulates.*
*She pushes forward. Another step. Another.*

`SFX: sand_increasing — cascade volume rises steadily`
`MUSIC: T03_collapse begins — low cello, descending`

---

**BEAT 7** `00.50–00.56`

*The sand has passed the halfway mark.*
*Sandy's upper bulb — now her head — is half full of gold sand pressing down.*

*Her steps slow.*
*Her cord arms tremble slightly under the weight.*
*She turns her head — impossible, the bulb is round — trying to see.*
*She cannot. The sand occludes everything.*

*The weight is now extreme. Each glove placement is an effort.*
*The polished floor shows her reflection sinking toward it.*

*She stops.*

*The last thing she sees through the sand: her own white gloves on the floor.*

`SFX: sand_increasing — maximum volume`
`MUSIC: cello pitch drops, slows, stops`

---

### SCENE 5 — THE FLOOR
**Location:** `club_interior`
**Timecode:** 00.56–01.00

---

**BEAT 8** `00.56–01.00`

*Sandy collapses.*

*She goes flat — upper bulb (full, heavy, crushed) pressed against the dark floor.*
*The cord arms spread at her sides. The white gloves face upward.*
*The cord legs are still in the air for one beat — then settle sideways.*

*All the gold sand has arrived at the lowest point.*
*She is pinned.*

`SFX: 00.56 — sandy_collapse: heavy bass thud + sand settling`
`SFX: 00.58 — crowd_reaction: ambient murmur shift, then settle`
`MUSIC: single low bass thud. Then silence.`

*The polished floor reflects her back at herself:*
*a flattened hourglass, golden and still, upside down.*

*HOLD 2 SECONDS.*

*Through the still-open club door behind her:*
*Inspector-Stopwatch. His clockface catches the spotlight from the exterior.*
*Arrow-brow: 6 o'clock.*

*He was never wrong.*

*FADE TO BLACK.*
